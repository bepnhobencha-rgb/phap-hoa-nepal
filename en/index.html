Poster

Open letter

Dear venerable monastics, Buddhist friends, neighbours and benefactors,

Phap Hoa Monastery in Surrey, British Columbia, warmly invites you to the fundraising programme “A Heart for Nepal.”

The programme is on Saturday, October 17, 2026, from 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM Pacific Time, at 18847 80 Avenue, Surrey, BC. It includes music and a vegetarian buffet. Please contact the organising committee to confirm admission details.

With compassion and a spirit of generosity, the programme aims to support people in Nepal affected by a natural disaster.

Contributions are made to Dharma Lotus Buddhist Cultural Society (charity registration number 776344152 RR 0001, effective July 12, 2026). Contributions will be used for charitable activities within the Society’s registered purposes, as the Society decides. The Society will report on the use of funds as applicable. This letter does not say that money is passed straight to individuals.

Whether an official donation receipt will be issued depends on CRA requirements and the Society’s confirmed policy. This letter is not a receipt.

On the programme poster, TT. Hạnh Từ, Tsengdok Rinpoche and Ven. Sooksunti are listed under Chứng minh. Ni trưởng Thích Nữ Minh An chairs the organising committee. The poster lists NS. Chúc Minh as trụ trì of Giac Vien Temple in Seattle, head of the food committee, and sponsor of the vegetarian meal. The poster lists SC. Diệu Sơn as MC.

Please register each person in advance so the committee can prepare seating, food and parking. Gifts are voluntary. You may give even if you cannot attend. There is no set amount.

With our warm wishes,

Organising committee, A Heart for Nepal
Phap Hoa Monastery, Surrey, BC
